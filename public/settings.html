Answer keys

A second key for OK, for cancel and for undo, beside Enter, Escape and ctrl+z, for a hand that is already on the keyboard. Click a box and press the key you want. Leave one empty to turn it off; the originals keep working either way. A key that the table already uses for something else is yours once you set it here.

Option keys

When the game offers a choice — Attack, Thwart, Change Form — these take them in the order they are shown, and each key is drawn on its option at the table. Click a box and press the key you want, or leave one empty to give that position no key.
